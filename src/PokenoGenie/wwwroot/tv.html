    YOUR GAME NIGHT GUIDE

    How to use Pokeno Genie.

    One caller, one big screen, and your physical Pokeno boards.

    1. 01

      Connect the two screens

      On the phone, tap Create a table. On the TV, open this site's tv.html page and enter the phone's eight-character room code.

    2. 02

      Pick the winning shape

      Choose a pattern and check its 5×5 preview. Tap Shuffle & start round for a fresh 52-card deck. The pattern stays fixed until the round ends.

    3. 03

      Draw, call, and mark

      Tap Draw card and call its rank and suit. Players mark matching cards on their boards. Wait three seconds between draws. The TV shows the current card and previous four.

    4. 04

      Someone calls “Pokeno!”

      Tap Pause / verify Pokeno. Select the saved set and physical board number, then Check board for a win. A winning shape is highlighted on both screens with a themed TV celebration. Confirm the physical board, then Resume drawing or End round. Use Board library to enter or generate boards before play.

    5. 05

      Make the table yours

      Choose a look in Card deck. The cards and background change on the phone and TV together. Your round, pattern, and called cards stay the same.

    6. 06

      Play another round

      After ending a round, pick a pattern and shuffle again using the same code. Pass the caller phone to the next person. End table & start new creates a new code and resets the round count.

    Drawing is on hold. The caller taps Return to table on the phone to close this guide on both screens.
