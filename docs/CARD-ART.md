# Bundled card artwork

The illustrations are local SVG assets generated by `node tools/generate-card-art.mjs`. No image service, external artwork request, or paid subscription is used at runtime.

## MOPAR Muscle

The collection contains four separate vehicle drawings. Each appears on 13 of the 52 cards; every suit includes all four models. The back shows the complete lineup. Numbered cards keep their correct pip counts, and court cards use car artwork with Jack, Queen, or King labels.

- **1969 Dodge Charger R/T:** long body, recessed fastback rear window, split concealed-headlight grille, rear bumblebee stripe.
- **1970 Dodge Challenger R/T:** shorter deck, vinyl roof, four headlights, R/T side stripe.
- **1970 Plymouth ’Cuda:** two headlights, shaker hood, rear hockey stick stripe.
- **1970 Plymouth Superbird:** pointed aero nose, black roof, and raised rear wing.

These are stylized original vector illustrations, rather than vehicle photographs. The separate 1969 Dart deck keeps its blue hardtop drawing.

### Logo and reference sources

The MOPAR emblem and wordmark use the paths from the [official Mopar header logo](https://www.mopar.com/content/dam/mopar/images/header/logos/mopar_logo.svg), downloaded from [Mopar](https://www.mopar.com/en-us.html). The unmodified source is retained at `tools/assets/mopar-logo.svg`. The generator extracts the emblem and wordmark, preserving their proportions and official blue, and places them on a light background for contrast.

Vehicle references include [Dodge's 1970 Challenger history](https://www.dodge.com/news/black-ghost-dodge-challenger.html), [Stellantis' 1970 Plymouth Superbird feature](https://blog.stellantisnorthamerica.com/2015/07/21/1970-superbird-travels-from-indiana-to-alaska/), and [Holley's 1970 ’Cuda shaker hood feature](https://www.holley.com/blog/post/unearthed_1970_plymouth_cuda_340_with_the_shaker_scoop/).

Mopar, Dodge, Plymouth and the vehicle names and logos belong to their respective owners. This is a fan themed deck, with no claim of affiliation or endorsement.

## Character collections

Harry Potter, Star Wars, and Lord of the Rings each have 52 faces and a matching card back. The artwork is original, stylized SVG illustration, generated from the editable sources in `tools/character-art/` and `tools/story-card-art.mjs`. No movie stills, actor photographs, remote image requests, or image generation services are needed to build or run the app.

The courts use mirrored character portraits, with these assignments in all four suits:

| Deck | King | Queen | Jack | Ace |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Harry Potter | Albus Dumbledore | Minerva McGonagall | Harry Potter | Golden Snitch |
| Star Wars | Darth Vader | Leia Organa | Luke Skywalker | Death Star |
| Lord of the Rings | Aragorn | Arwen | Frodo Baggins | One Ring |

The 2–10 cards feature nine supporting characters per collection, distributed deterministically across the four suits. This gives artwork variety without introducing randomness or changing the shuffled deck, call history, or rules. They use full character scenes instead of central pip arrays; corner ranks and standard suit symbols identify the cards from either end.

- **Harry Potter:** Hermione, Ron, Hagrid, Luna, Dobby, Snape, Sirius, Draco, and Hedwig. Parchment, castle silhouettes, wand borders, and spell flourishes frame the portraits.
- **Star Wars:** Obi-Wan, Yoda, Rey, Ahsoka, Han, Chewbacca, R2-D2, C-3PO, and the Mandalorian. Vader has red lightsaber rails, Luke and Yoda green, Obi-Wan blue, Rey yellow, and Ahsoka white. Other characters use spacecraft panel framing against a star field.
- **Lord of the Rings:** Gandalf, Legolas, Gimli, Sam, Galadriel, Eowyn, Boromir, Gollum, and Sauron. Elven leaf borders, map contours, mountains, swords, and character accessories form part of the illustration.

Character design references include the official [Harry Potter encyclopedia](https://www.harrypotter.com/fact-file/characters-and-pets/albus-dumbledore), the [Star Wars Databank](https://www.starwars.com/databank/leia-organa), and its [Vader](https://www.starwars.com/databank/darth-vaders-lightsaber) and [Luke](https://www.starwars.com/databank/luke-skywalkers-lightsaber) lightsaber entries. The Lord of the Rings court assignments follow the requested Aragorn/Arwen/Frodo/One Ring arrangement.

These are fan themed collections; the characters, franchise names, and associated marks belong to their respective owners. No affiliation or endorsement is claimed.

## Royal Cats

The Royal Cats collection uses original flat vector illustrations based on the supplied cat photos. Their names and assignments are the same in all four suits:

| Rank | Cat | Identifying details |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Jack | Binx | Black coat, yellow-green eyes and a small white chest locket. |
| Queen | Autumn | Mostly black face, orange cheek and coat patches, white chest, green eyes and a tiara. |
| King | Chip | Grey cap and shoulders, broad white face blaze and chest, pink nose, folded ear, wink and a crown. |
| Ace | Anya | White face and chest, orange cheeks, black crown patches and the distinctive black-tipped nose. |

The Jack, Queen and King portraits are mirrored like the other character courts. The Ace shows Anya in an ornamental frame. Number cards alternate among nine scenes: yarn, a basket nap, a window watch, a box, stretching, a book, a fish toy, a garden and moonlight. Corner ranks and standard suits still identify every card.

`tools/cat-card-art.mjs` contains the editable portrait, scene, back and background definitions. The back and the moonlit sitting-room background reuse the same four pet illustrations. The supplied photos are references only and are not included in the public repository or deployed app.

## Matching backgrounds

Every alternate deck has a bundled 1600 × 900 SVG background shared by the caller, Blazor display, and lightweight TV display. The caller's shuffle and drawing panels also show the selected scenery behind the deck, with light labels and contrasting controls. The Illustrated deck retains its original green table and cream panels. Backgrounds follow the card collection automatically, including during verification; no external image service is used.

- **Midnight:** navy, gold constellations and Art Deco arcs.
- **Sunburst:** coral sun rays and blue rolling shapes.
- **MOPAR Muscle:** a garage with the same four model illustrations used in the deck and the MOPAR emblem.
- **1969 Dart:** a blue and chrome garage with the deck's blue hardtop.
- **Harry Potter:** a moonlit castle, an owl and wand magic.
- **Star Wars:** a battle station, starfighters and a star field in the cards' vector style.
- **Lord of the Rings:** mountains, Elven woodland, the White Tree and a golden ring.
- **Royal Cats:** a moonlit sitting room, plants, paw prints and all four named cats.

Run `node tools/generate-deck-backgrounds.mjs` to regenerate them after generating card art. The automotive backgrounds reuse the deck's existing vehicle symbols. The assets are in `src/PokenoGenie/wwwroot/backgrounds/`; their colors and overlays are controlled by `css/deck-backgrounds.css`.
